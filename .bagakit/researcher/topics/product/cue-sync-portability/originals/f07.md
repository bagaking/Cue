## Why It Matters

Provides lower-level same-filesystem rename guarantees and RENAME_SWAP semantics that bound, but do not identify, Foundation behavior.

## Retained Contract Excerpts

- Darwin says `rename` replaces an existing same-type destination on the same
  filesystem and guarantees an instance of the destination name exists across
  a crash during the operation.
- `RENAME_SWAP`, only on filesystems advertising support, atomically swaps
  source and destination names.
- These are contracts of `rename`/`renamex_np`; Apple Foundation documentation
  does not say that `replaceItemAt` is implemented by either call.

## Limitations
- The manual documents Darwin syscalls, not FileManager.replaceItemAt implementation; Cue does not call rename or renamex_np directly.
