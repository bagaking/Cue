## Track Contract

- track id: `copper-discussion`
- parent pass: `pass-001`
- parent charter: `charter.md`

## Track Question

collect Copper owner posts and attributable public user replies

## Required Source Types
- The supplied Copper launch/update post and any publicly attributable replies.
- At least one product-owner clarification and one user-authored request or
  failure story when accessible.

## Preferred Sources
- X post/reply URLs with author, timestamp, and verbatim relevant excerpt.
- Public mirrors or search indexes only as access aids; preserve the canonical
  X URL whenever known.

## Disallowed Sources
- Anonymous paraphrases, AI summaries, and owner marketing treated as user
  demand.

## Source Id Range

`x01` through `x07`.

## Owned Output Files
- tracks/copper-discussion.md
- originals/x01.md through originals/x07.md
- summaries/x01.md through summaries/x07.md

## Minimum Evidence

Retain the supplied post plus enough replies to distinguish explicit demand
from praise, speculation, or owner roadmap claims. If replies are inaccessible,
record the exact access boundary and do not infer demand.

## Lead Policy

Follow only reply-chain or author-profile leads that may materially change sync,
mobile, portability, attachment, conflict, offline, or privacy requirements.

## Drift Check

Every retained excerpt must answer a Cue product requirement; visual praise or
generic launch reactions alone are out of scope.

## Merge Notes
- Do not edit shared claims, insights, proposal, Tracker, or product code.
