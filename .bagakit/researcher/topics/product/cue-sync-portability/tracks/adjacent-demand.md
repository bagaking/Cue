## Track Question

collect comparable-tool user evidence for sync mobile attachments conflicts offline and privacy

## Required Source Types
- Attributable public GitHub issues, forum posts, or reviews from at least two
  comparable local-first capture or notes products.

## Preferred Sources
- Concrete user requests or failure reports covering sync, offline edits,
  conflicts/deletion, attachments, export, mobile capture, or privacy.

## Disallowed Sources
- Vendor feature grids without user discussion; affiliate comparison pages;
  unattributed SEO summaries.

## Source Id Range

`j01` through `j04`.

## Owned Output Files
- tracks/adjacent-demand.md
- originals/j01.md through originals/j04.md
- summaries/j01.md through summaries/j04.md

## Minimum Evidence

At least four retained user-authored sources spanning two products and three
requirement dimensions, with counterevidence or coverage limitations noted.

## Lead Policy

Follow one high-value linked issue or reply chain per product; stop when the
next source repeats an already-supported requirement without new failure detail.

## Drift Check

Evidence must support demand or failure modes, not merely prove that a product
ships a feature.
